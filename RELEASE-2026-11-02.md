# ZoneLevelUp 2.0.0-beta29-r50

## English
- Cumulative r48-r50 update: per-character rebirth progression, safer store purchases and pending delivery handling, and global Atlas/store search.
- Level, milestone, achievement, title and rebirth celebrations, with a bounded queue and reduced-motion controls. Celebrations do not grant extra rewards.
- GODIELAND: El Pacto del Alba contains 24 cinematic chapters. Valid rebirths unlock chapters; history and replay are checked per character.
- Press K to open the Atlas, then HERO / GODIELAND, or use /zcine. Server rules control progression and cinematic settings.
- Includes 72 offline Spanish narration clips synthesized with Piper es_MX-claude-high. Narration is synthetic, not a human recording. Closing, skipping, taking damage or changing dimension stops narration and returns control.
- IMPORTANT: client and server must BOTH use beta29-r50 (protocol 54 / Journey1). Do not mix this release with r47, other r revisions or beta30.
