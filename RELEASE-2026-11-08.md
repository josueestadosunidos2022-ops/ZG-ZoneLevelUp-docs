## Rebirth chronicle / Crónica de renacer

The 24-chapter Pacto del Alba follows Mara, the alliance of ten peoples and the confrontation with Azhrak. Each chapter contains three acts, with Spanish narration, subtitles and timed 3D dioramas. Three original instrumental themes accompany the story: Memoria, Asedio and Alba. Chapter indices and existing unlocks remain unchanged. / Los 24 capítulos conservan índices/desbloqueos y relatan alianza y enfrentamiento en tres actos cada uno, con narración española, subtítulos y dioramas 3D. Tres temas originales acompañan la crónica.

Narration and music are finished local audio assets; gameplay does not need an online voice service. Text/audio matching prevents obsolete audio from speaking a changed script; mismatched audio falls back to text with reading time. Music ducks while narration plays. / Audio final local, sin servicio online. Si cambia el texto y no coincide el audio, muestra texto con tiempo de lectura; música baja durante narración.

## Class awakening and encounter continuity / Despertar y continuidad

Eight combat classes each gain three configurable awakening tiers. They amplify existing passives and class-specific health/armor, not professions or extra races. A passive6% with multiplier1.5 becomes9%, not50% extra total damage. Existing caps and external-mod requirements remain. / Ocho clases con tres rangos; amplían pasivas y vida/armadura.6%×1.5=9%, no50% de daño total; conserva límites y requisitos externos.

Healing another injured player, class-specific kills/hits, claimed contracts, recognized boss victories and completed encounters contribute only when applicable to the active class. Repeating already-claimed rewards does not add counters. / Acciones válidas cuentan para clase activa; recompensas ya cobradas no vuelven a contar.

Expedition death/return retains the original session, entry charge and end time. Voluntary exit is `/zsalir`. The system does not duplicate inventory or replace Corail rules; protections do not cover sources that bypass invulnerability. / Morir/volver conserva sesión/cobro/plazo, salida `/zsalir`, sin duplicar inventario ni sustituir Corail; daño que ignora invulnerabilidad queda fuera.
